# Robotic Arm Simulation — SolidWorks 2022
Internship: CodeAlpha | Robotics & Automation
Batch: 10th May 2026 – 10th June 2026

## Task Overview
Full 6-DOF robotic arm designed and simulated
from scratch in SolidWorks 2022 Premium.

## What Was Built
- 10 individual parts modeled
- Full constrained assembly
- Gear-driven gripper end effector
- Pick and place motion simulation

## Degrees of Freedom
- DOF 1: Base rotation
- DOF 2: Shoulder joint
- DOF 3: Elbow with gear mechanism
- DOF 4: Wrist rotation
- DOF 5 & 6: Gripper fingers

## Tools Used
- SolidWorks 2022 Premium
